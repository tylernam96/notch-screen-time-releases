Refunds

Refund Policy

Effective 8 October 2026

If NotchUsage doesn't fit how you work, email within 14 days of buying and you'll get a full refund. No questions asked.

How to ask

Email support@notchusage.example from the address you bought with, or include your order number from the Dodo Payments receipt. You don't need to give a reason.

What happens next

  • The refund is issued through Dodo Payments, which processed your purchase, to the card or account you paid with, usually within a few business days. Your bank may take a little longer to show it.
  • The license key is revoked, and at its next license check NotchUsage locks: nudges, the notch glance and the analysis window stop working. Your recorded history stays on your Mac until you delete it (see how).

After 14 days

Purchases are final after 14 days, but if something's wrong, write anyway; we'd rather fix it. This doesn't limit any refund rights you have under the consumer laws where you live.
